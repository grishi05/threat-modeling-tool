OWASP Top 10 , Know the Threats

Every web and AI application is vulnerable to some combination of these 10 risk categories. After your analysis, refer back here to understand the real-world impact of any identified vulnerability.

A01
Broken Access Control
Users act outside intended permissions , attackers access other users' accounts, view sensitive data, or perform admin functions. Most common OWASP risk since 2021.
A02
Cryptographic Failures
Sensitive data (passwords, credit cards, health records) exposed due to weak or missing encryption in transit or at rest.
A03
Injection
Malicious data sent as a command or query , SQL, NoSQL, OS, and LDAP injection. Attackers can steal, modify, or destroy your entire database.
A04
Insecure Design
Flaws baked into the architecture itself , not just code bugs. Missing threat modeling, insecure design patterns, and lack of security requirements at design time.
A05
Security Misconfiguration
Default credentials, unnecessary features enabled, missing patches, overly permissive cloud storage. The broadest category , affects 90%+ of applications.
A06
Vulnerable & Outdated Components
Using libraries, frameworks, or dependencies with known vulnerabilities. A single outdated package can expose your entire app (see: Log4Shell).
A07
Identification & Auth Failures
Weak passwords, missing MFA, exposed session tokens, credential stuffing. Allows attackers to assume other users' identities.
A08
Software & Data Integrity Failures
Untrusted plugins, libraries, or CI/CD pipeline steps. Includes insecure deserialization , attackers can execute arbitrary code via malicious data objects.
A09
Security Logging & Monitoring Failures
Without proper logs and alerts, breaches go undetected for months. Average breach detection time is 204 days , logs are your only forensic evidence.
A10
Server-Side Request Forgery (SSRF)
Attacker tricks the server into making requests to internal systems , used to access cloud metadata, internal APIs, or pivot deeper into your infrastructure.
